Privacy and community safety

Student Connect pilot notice · Effective 10 September 2026

Student Connect is in a limited pilot. University approval and an official support address are required before wider campus access.

Information we use

We use your verified university email, profile details, posts, comments, reactions, club and event activity, reports, and device notification token to operate and protect the campus community.

Visibility and providers

Campus members can see profiles and published content. Reports, blocks, device tokens, saved posts, and personal notifications remain private. Authorized moderators can review reports and related content. Supabase stores identity and application data. Firebase provides hosting, diagnostics, analytics, and device messaging. Student Connect does not sell student data.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while an account is active. In-app account deletion removes the profile, posts, comments, registrations, and uploaded media. De-identified moderation records and security logs may be retained for up to 24 months to address abuse and protect the service.

Acceptable use and reports

Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, publish private information without permission, post unlawful material, infringe intellectual property, bypass access controls, or disrupt the service. Use the in-app report action for suspected violations. For an immediate threat, contact university security or local emergency services.

Questions and requests

Use the in-app reporting and account controls or contact your campus administrator. An official support email will be published before the pilot expands.
